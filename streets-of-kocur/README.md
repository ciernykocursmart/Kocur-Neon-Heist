# STREETS OF KOCUR

A 2D side-scrolling beat 'em up by **Kocur Studios**. You are **Kocur**, a tough
black alley cat with a red scarf. The **White Crown** gang of white cats has taken
over the neighbourhood and stolen the food supplies. Fight through six streets,
beat three lieutenants and take down the gang boss, the enormous **Lord Lardpaw**.

* **Engine:** Godot 4.3 (GDScript, Compatibility / OpenGL 3.3 renderer)
* **Platform:** Windows 10/11 x64 (the project also runs on Linux)
* **Campaign:** 6 streets, 5 enemy types, 3 mini-bosses, 1 final boss, ending
* **Replay:** score and rank per street, 3 difficulties, a hidden Golden Fish and an
  optional challenge in every street
* **Assets:** all art is drawn in code (`_draw`), all sound effects and music are
  generated by `tools/gen_audio.py`. No third-party art, audio or fonts.

---

## Launching the Windows build

1. Unzip `StreetsOfKocur-windows-x86_64.zip` anywhere.
2. Double-click `StreetsOfKocur/StreetsOfKocur.exe`.
3. The .exe is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may say "unknown publisher":
   click **More info -> Run anyway**.

Requirements: Windows 10/11 64-bit, a GPU with OpenGL 3.3, keyboard or an
Xbox-style gamepad. Works offline, nothing to install.

Saves: `%APPDATA%\StreetsOfKocur\streets_save.json` (progress) and
`%APPDATA%\StreetsOfKocur\settings.cfg` (audio and display).

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Walk / move along the street | A D W S or arrow keys | left stick / D-pad |
| Run | double-tap a direction, or hold Shift | RB |
| Jump | Space | A |
| Attack (combo) | J | X |
| PAWQUAKE special | K | Y |
| Grab / throw | E | B |
| Pause | Esc or P | Start |

Menus: arrows/stick + Enter/J (confirm), Esc/K (back), or the mouse.
The full list is also in **Main menu -> Controls** and in the pause menu.

## How to play

**Street and depth.** The street is a pseudo-3D plane: left/right is the street,
up/down is depth. Attacks only hit cats on (almost) the same depth line, so line
up with your target. Shadows show where everyone really stands.

**Moves**

* **Combo** `J J J`: paw jab -> claw swipe -> heavy WHAM that knocks down. Press
  during or just after a hit to continue; wait too long and the combo restarts.
  The finisher always ends the chain, so mashing does not loop forever.
* **Flying kick** `Space` then `J`. Jumping also dodges bottles, shockwaves and low
  attacks.
* **Pounce**: attack while running.
* **PAWQUAKE** `K`: a spinning blast around Kocur that knocks down everyone close.
  Kocur is invulnerable during the spin. Costs one of the two blue energy bars;
  energy refills when you land hits, when you get hit, and slowly over time.
* **Grab** `E` next to a gang cat: `J` = knee (3 knees end in a throw), `E` = throw
  (hold a direction to aim). Thrown cats bowl over their friends. Enforcers and
  bosses cannot be grabbed.

**Reading the gang.** A yellow **!** over a cat means it is about to attack. A red
zone on the ground marks where a big hit will land. Bosses show **OPEN!** when
they are tired after a big move.

**Health, lives and continues.** After a hit Kocur blinks and is briefly
invulnerable; three hits in a row always knock him down so you cannot be
stun-locked. Losing all health costs a life and Kocur gets up where he fell. With
no lives left it is Game Over: a **continue** restarts from the last checkpoint
(every cleared fight is a checkpoint) with full lives. Eaten food and broken props
stay gone after a continue.

| Difficulty | Enemy damage | Attackers at once | Lives | Continues per street | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Easy | 60% | 1 | 5 | unlimited | x0.75 |
| Normal | 100% | 2 | 3 | 3 | x1 |
| Hard | 130%, +25% enemy health | 3 | 2 | 2 | x1.5 |

**Food.** Fish +20, chicken +40, cat food can +70 health (max 100). Eating at full
health gives 200 points instead. Smash crates, barrels, trash cans and market
stalls: some hide food, and every street hides one **Golden Fish** (+3000 points
and a full special meter). Each street has only a few healing items.

**Score.** Defeated enemies, every hit, combos of 5+ hits, bosses, items, broken
props, plus time, health and no-damage bonuses on the results screen. An extra
life every 40,000 points (max 9 lives). Each street gives a rank S-D and has an
optional challenge (for example "Land an 8-hit combo").

## The campaign

| # | Street | What is new | End |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Alley Trouble | tutorial hints, punks and a brawler, first food | gang ambush |
| 2 | Market Mayhem | runners, breakable stalls, more mixed groups | big market brawl |
| 3 | Neon Boulevard | night city, throwers, traffic barriers, electric puddles | lieutenant **Slick Whiskers** |
| 4 | Industrial Paws | enforcers, crate walls, hydraulic presses | mini-boss **Big Bruno Boltpaw** |
| 5 | White Crown Headquarters | six named areas mixing everything | **General Snowmane** |
| 6 | The Fat Cat Showdown | bodyguards in the gang's pantry | **Lord Lardpaw** (3 phases), ending |

Bosses:

* **Slick Whiskers** - dash (double dash in phase 2), fishbone fan, claw combo;
  calls punks at 50%.
* **Big Bruno Boltpaw** - wrench slam with a ground shockwave (jump it), shoulder
  charge that leaves him dizzy if he hits a wall, low sweep; calls runners at 50%.
* **General Snowmane** - lunge, spear Banner Spin, Crown Leap (shadow marks the landing); reinforcements at 66% and 33%.
* **Lord Lardpaw** - Belly Bounce (marked landing + shock ring), Rolling Charge,
  Paw Slam; bodyguards at 66%; **Food Frenzy** at 33% (he stops to gobble a
  drumstick, then gets faster and cans rain from the shelves).

## Opening and building the project

* Install **Godot 4.3** (standard, not .NET). Open `streets-of-kocur/project.godot`.
  The main scene is `scenes/main_menu.tscn`.
* The game sounds are already in `assets/audio/`. To regenerate them:
  `python3 tools/gen_audio.py` (needs numpy).
* **Tests:** `tools/run_tests.sh` (headless; add suite names to run only some, e.g.
  `tools/run_tests.sh combat food`). It also runs a two-process save/restart check.
* **Bot playthrough / balance:** `godot --headless --path . -s res://tests/sim.gd -- all normal`
* **Windows build:** install the 4.3.stable export templates, then
  `tools/build_windows.sh`. It writes `build/windows/StreetsOfKocur.exe` and
  `build/windows/StreetsOfKocur-windows-x86_64.zip`, then smoke-tests the exported
  pack (see TEST_REPORT.md). It needs Node.js to put the Kocur icon on the .exe.
* **Microsoft Store .msix:** `tools/build_msix.sh path/to/makemsix` (after the Windows
  build); Store images: `tools/store_shots.sh`.
* **Screenshots:** `xvfb-run -a -s "-screen 0 1280x720x24" godot --path . --resolution 1280x720 -s res://tests/shot.gd -- OUT_DIR menu gallery level:0:1050:punk,brawler:110`

## Project layout

```
project.godot            Godot project (autoloads, 1280x720, 60 physics ticks)
scenes/                  main_menu.tscn, level.tscn, ending.tscn
scripts/autoload/        settings, save_manager, game (run + progress), audio_manager, transition
scripts/data/            balance.gd (all tuning), levels.gd (6 streets), looks.gd (cat designs)
scripts/combat/          fighter.gd (shared body/hit logic), projectile.gd
scripts/actors/          player.gd, enemy.gd, enemies/{punk,brawler,runner,thrower,enforcer}.gd
scripts/bosses/          boss.gd, slick.gd, bruno.gd, snowmane.gd, lardpaw.gd
scripts/level/           level.gd (stage, loop, scoring), encounter_manager.gd, pickup.gd, prop.gd, hazard.gd
scripts/gfx/             cat_painter.gd, stage_painter.gd, pickup_art.gd, fx.gd
scripts/ui/              main_menu, hud, level_overlays (pause/game over/results), settings_panel, ending, ui_kit
scripts/debug/           autoplay_bot.gd (scripted player for tests only)
assets/audio/            39 sound effects + 9 music loops (generated WAV)
tests/                   run_tests.gd, test_case.gd, suites/, restart_check.gd, sim.gd, shot.gd
tools/                   gen_audio.py, run_tests.sh, build_windows.sh, pack_smoke.gd,
                         patch_windows_exe.mjs (exe icon), build_msix.sh, store_shots.sh, store_art.gd
store/                   Microsoft Store kit (manifest, logos, art, screenshots, listing)
build/PLAYER_README.txt  README shipped inside the ZIP
```

How it fits together: `Level` runs a fixed 60 Hz loop and ticks the player, enemies,
projectiles, hazards and pickups in a fixed order. Every attack activation is a
dictionary with a unique id and its own "already hit" list, so a hitbox damages
each target at most once. Hit tests use ground position + depth + height
(`Fighter.overlaps`), never sprite overlap. `EncounterManager` locks the camera,
spawns staggered waves (max 5 alive), keeps enemies inside the arena and clears
the fight only when everyone is down.
