### Description [up to 10,000 characters]
Streets of Kocur is a classic 2D side-scrolling beat 'em up. You are Kocur, a
black alley cat with a red scarf and a short temper. The White Crown gang, a
crew of white cats led by the enormous Lord Lardpaw, has taken over the
neighbourhood and emptied every fish stall and pantry. One cat is enough to
take it all back.

Fight along a pseudo-3D street where you move left, right and in depth. Line up
with your target, read the gang's warning signs and hit hard:
- Chain a three-hit combo that ends in a heavy knock-down.
- Jump into a flying kick, or pounce at a run.
- Grab a gang cat, knee it and throw it into its friends.
- Unleash PAWQUAKE, a spinning blast that knocks down everyone close.

Six streets, each with its own look and new tricks:
- **Alley Trouble:** learn the basics against punks and brawlers.
- **Market Mayhem:** fast runners and breakable market stalls.
- **Neon Boulevard:** bottle throwers, traffic barriers and electric puddles.
- **Industrial Paws:** armoured enforcers and hydraulic presses.
- **White Crown Headquarters:** every trick the gang knows, in six areas.
- **The Fat Cat Showdown:** the gang's pantry and its boss.

Four boss fights test what you have learned:
- **Slick Whiskers** dashes and throws fishbone fans.
- **Big Bruno Boltpaw** slams his wrench into shockwaves you must jump.
- **General Snowmane** spins his banner spear and leaps from above.
- **Lord Lardpaw** belly-bounces, rolls across the room and, at the end, stops
  to gobble a drumstick before his Food Frenzy.

Smash crates, barrels and trash cans for fish, chicken and cat food, hunt the
hidden Golden Fish in every street, and chase an S rank, a high score and the
optional challenge of each street.

- 6 streets, 5 gang enemy types, 3 mini-bosses and a 3-phase final boss
- Combos, flying kicks, running pounces, grabs, throws and the PAWQUAKE special
- Readable fights: attack warnings, marked landing zones and boss "OPEN!" moments
- Food to heal, breakable props, a hidden Golden Fish and a challenge in every street
- Score, combo counter, extra lives and an S-D rank for every street
- Easy, Normal and Hard difficulty, with continues from checkpoints
- Keyboard or Xbox-style gamepad
- Original hand-drawn style art, music and sound effects
- Single player, fully offline; no account, no ads, no in-game purchases

## runFullTrust justification (if Partner Center asks)
Streets of Kocur is a desktop game built with the Godot Engine. It ships as a
Win32 executable (StreetsOfKocur.exe) packaged in MSIX, and a desktop (Win32)
application needs runFullTrust to run. The game does not access the network,
other apps' data or any personal data. It only reads and writes its own save and
settings files in %APPDATA%\StreetsOfKocur.

## Notes for certification (Submission options)
Offline single-player game. No account, login or network access is required.

Controls:
- Keyboard: A D W S or arrow keys move, Shift or double-tap runs, Space jumps,
  J attacks, K special, E grabs/throws, Esc or P pauses. Menus: arrows + Enter, Esc back.
- Gamepad: left stick / D-pad move, A jump, X attack, Y special, B grab, RB run,
  Start pause.
