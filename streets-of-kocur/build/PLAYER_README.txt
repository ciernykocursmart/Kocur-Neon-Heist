STREETS OF KOCUR  v1.0.0  -  a Kocur Studios game
=================================================

A 2D side-scrolling beat 'em up. You are Kocur, a black alley cat with a red
scarf. The White Crown gang of white cats has taken over the city - fight
through six streets and take down their boss, the enormous Lord Lardpaw.

HOW TO START
  1. Unzip the whole folder anywhere (for example to your Desktop).
  2. Double-click StreetsOfKocur.exe.
  Windows SmartScreen may warn about an unknown publisher because the
  executable is not code-signed: click "More info" -> "Run anyway".

REQUIREMENTS
  Windows 10/11 64-bit, a graphics card with OpenGL 3.3 support.
  Keyboard, or an Xbox-style gamepad. No internet or installation needed.

CONTROLS            KEYBOARD                        GAMEPAD
  Walk / move       A D W S  or arrow keys          left stick / D-pad
  Run               double-tap a direction or Shift RB
  Jump              Space                           A
  Attack (combo)    J   (J J J = paw, claw, WHAM)   X
  PAWQUAKE special  K   (costs one energy bar)      Y
  Grab / throw      E   (J while holding = knee)    B
  Pause             Esc or P                        Start
  Jump + J = flying kick. Run + J = pounce.

TIPS
  A yellow "!" over a gang cat = it is about to attack.
  A red zone on the ground = a big hit lands there - get out.
  Bosses show "OPEN!" when they are vulnerable after a big move.
  Smash crates, barrels and stalls: they hide food and one Golden Fish
  per street. Fish +20, chicken +40, cat food can +70 health.
  Extra life every 40,000 points. Each cleared fight is a checkpoint.

SAVES AND SETTINGS
  %APPDATA%\StreetsOfKocur\streets_save.json   (progress, best scores, difficulty)
  %APPDATA%\StreetsOfKocur\settings.cfg        (volume, display, screen shake)
  Progress is saved at the start of each street and after each clear.
  Reset progress from Main Menu -> Reset Progress.
