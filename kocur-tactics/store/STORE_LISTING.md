## English (United States)

### Product name
Kocur Tactics

### Short description [up to 1000 characters; shown at the top of the listing]
Lead a squad of black cats against the White Crown Legion. Plan every move on a
tile grid with exact damage previews, flanks, cover and line of sight, and
outsmart two telegraphing bosses across a 12-mission pixel-art campaign.

### Description [up to 10,000 characters]
The White Crown Legion has marched into Shadowwood Valley. Its white-cat
soldiers burn the orchards, lock up the villagers and serve the cold Pale King.
Only a handful of black cats stand in their way: Nox the Shadowblade, Bramble
the Guardian and Willow the Ranger.

Kocur Tactics is a turn-based tactics game in pixel art. Every hero moves once
and then acts once per turn. There are no dice: damage is always shown exactly
before you confirm. Victory comes from good positioning:
- Flank an enemy with two heroes for bonus melee damage.
- Hide in forests for cover.
- Use walls to block archers' line of sight.
- Take the high ground.
- Press T at any time to see every tile the enemy can reach next turn.

The campaign has 12 missions in 3 acts, and no two missions play the same:
- Eliminate the enemy at Mill Bridge.
- Rescue Moss the healer in Mosswell.
- Hold the Shadowwood Shrine.
- Slip over the Border Wall.
- Protect the Moonwell.
- Free prisoners in the Border Keep.
- Survive the Burning Orchard.
- Escort the Elder.
- Claim relics at the Pale Gate.

Two boss battles test everything you have learned:
- **Ironpaw Commander.** He telegraphs his Crushing Blow, so move out of the
  marked tiles. Then punish him while he is exhausted.
- **The Pale King.** He hides behind Crown Pylons, fires a Royal Lance down whole
  rows and, in his second phase, unleashes the Pale Nova.

Between battles your cats earn XP and level up. Spend Relic Shards on five
upgrades per hero, including a class mastery. Recruit new heroes, chase 24
optional objectives and Gold medals, and test yourself in three tactical
challenges.

- 12 campaign missions in 3 acts, plus 3 tactical challenges
- 4 hero classes: Shadowblade, Guardian, Ranger and Mender
- 5 enemy types and 2 multi-phase bosses with telegraphed attacks
- Exact damage previews, flanking, cover, line of sight, hills and hazards
- Enemy threat overlay, undo move, and a clear log of every action
- Hero levels, 5 upgrades per hero, 3 recruitable heroes
- 24 optional objectives, Bronze/Silver/Gold medals and a records screen
- Story, Normal and Hard difficulty
- Original pixel art, music and sound
- Single player, fully offline; no account, no ads, no in-game purchases

## runFullTrust justification (if Partner Center asks)
Kocur Tactics is a desktop game built with the Godot Engine. It ships as a Win32
executable (KocurTactics.exe) packaged in MSIX, and a desktop (Win32) application
needs runFullTrust to run. The game does not access the network, other apps'
data or any personal data. It only reads and writes its own save and settings
files in %APPDATA%\KocurTactics.

## Notes for certification (Submission options)
Offline single-player game. No account, login or network access is required.

Controls:
- Mouse: left click selects a hero or moves to a blue tile. Clicking an enemy
  once aims, and clicking it again confirms. Right click cancels.
- Keyboard: 1-4 abilities, T enemy threat, Z undo move, Enter end turn, Esc pause.
