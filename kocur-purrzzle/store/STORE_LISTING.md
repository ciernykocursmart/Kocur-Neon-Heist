### Description [up to 10,000 characters]
Kocur Purrzzle is a cozy pixel-art puzzle game starring Kocur, a small black
cat with big golden eyes. Every room is a hand-made puzzle: cover every paw
target with a crate to open the glowing exit, then step through it.

Crates can only be pushed, never pulled, so every move counts. Luckily you can
undo any move with one key and restart a room whenever you like. Nothing is
ever timed and nothing can hurt you: it is all about thinking ahead.

Five regions, six puzzles each, every one teaching something new:
- **Sunny Garden:** learn to push crates and plan your route around hedges.
- **Moonlit Ruins:** stone plates open iron doors, and golden keys open locks.
- **Frozen Fishpond:** ice is slippery. You and the crates slide until something stops you.
- **Crystal Catacombs:** warp pads jump you across walls, and crystal gates open and close with every beat.
- **Purrfect Temple:** everything you have learned, combined, ending in the final puzzle.

Collect the hidden fish and solve each room within its move target to earn up
to three stars per level. Targets are generous, so a first solve always counts,
and replaying any level is one click away.

Features at a glance:
- 30 handcrafted levels, every one checked by a solver to be fair and solvable.
- Unlimited undo and instant restart.
- New mechanics are always introduced with a short hint first.
- A warning when a crate gets stuck in a corner.
- Charming pixel art and gentle music, all original.
- Keyboard and mouse controls, with an on-screen arrow pad.
- Progress and settings are saved automatically.
- Fully offline. No ads, no accounts, no in-app purchases.

## runFullTrust justification (if Partner Center asks)
Kocur Purrzzle is a desktop game built with the Godot Engine. It ships as a
Win32 executable (KocurPurrzzle.exe) packaged in MSIX, and a desktop (Win32)
application needs runFullTrust to run. The game does not access the network,

## Notes for certification (Submission options)
Offline single-player puzzle game. No account, login or network access is required.

Controls: WASD or arrow keys move, Z undoes a move, R restarts the level,
Space waits one beat, Esc pauses, F11 toggles fullscreen. Menus: arrow keys +
Enter, or the mouse. An on-screen arrow pad can be used with the mouse.
Level 1 starts unlocked; each finished level unlocks the next one.
