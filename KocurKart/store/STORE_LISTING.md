## Short description

## Description
Buckle up your whiskers! Kocur Kart is a colourful arcade kart racer from Kocur Studios that is easy to pick up and hard to put down.

DRIFT, BOOST, REPEAT
Hold drift into a corner and watch the sparks change colour. Release on blue for a small boost, or wait for orange and fire a big one. Every boosted drift fills your Purr meter, and you choose when to spend it. Nail the countdown for a perfect start, find the shortcuts and take your racing line from good to purr-fect.

SIX HANDCRAFTED TRACKS
• Whiskerwood Circuit – sweeping forest bends, a hairpin around the windmill and a hidden dirt path
• Purrport Pier – wooden piers with open edges above the harbour, and crabs that never look where they're going
• Neon Catway – a glowing night run across the rooftops with a leap between towers
• Frostpaw Pass – climb to the summit ice tunnel and keep your paws steady on frozen sections
• Sunset Safari – boulders, a dune jump and tumbleweeds under a burning sky
• Royal Catnip Run – the championship finale, with hairpins, a moat jump and the royal gate

THREE WAYS TO RACE
• Grand Prix – three two-race cups with points, standings and gold, silver or bronze trophies
• Single Race – any unlocked track, 1 to 5 laps, three difficulty levels
• Time Trial – race the clock and your own ghost for bronze, silver and gold medals

RIVALS WITH PERSONALITY
Five AI cats race by the same rules you do: no teleporting and no hidden catch-up boosts. Easy is relaxed, Normal makes natural mistakes, and Hard drifts, boosts and takes shortcuts.

MAKE IT YOURS
Unlock six playable cats (including Kocur, the black cat of Kocur Studios), three kart bodies with distinct handling, and paints, rims and accessories such as goggles, headphones and a royal crown. Every unlock tells you exactly how to earn it, with no grinding and no purchases.

PLAY YOUR WAY
Full keyboard and gamepad support, rebindable keys, adjustable camera, volume and display settings. Single player and fully offline: no account, no ads, no in-app purchases.
