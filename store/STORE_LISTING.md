## Description

Lumen City never sleeps, and neither does WARDEN — the security AI that runs every guard, drone and camera owned by ARGUS Consolidated.

You are KOCUR, Subject 07: a small black cat with neural implants who escaped an ARGUS lab. A mysterious fixer called MOTH pays you to rob ARGUS facilities one contract at a time. Every heist pulls another piece of a buried secret into the light, and leads you closer to CRADLE, the black site where it all began.

STEALTH OR STEEL — YOUR CALL
Every mission can be played your way. Stay in the shadows, read the guards' vision cones, slip past cameras and take enemies down silently from behind. Or pull out the Hailstorm SMG and the Thunderclaw shotgun and fight your way to the exit. Stealth pays better: alarms cut your fee and call in reinforcements, while a clean "ghost" run earns a big bonus.

HACK EVERYTHING
Security doors, cameras, terminals and alarm panels all have a quick timing-based breach. Loop the camera feeds, override the doors, skim corporate credits or switch off the motion sensors. But every missed beat makes noise.

A LIVING SECURITY SYSTEM
Guards hear footsteps and gunshots, investigate noises, find fallen colleagues and radio in alarms. Fast patrol drones, shielded Elite Hunters and armoured Enforcers with telegraphed laser rifles all demand different tactics. Kill a spotted guard before his radio call goes through, and the alarm never sounds.

A COMPLETE CAMPAIGN
- 12 missions in three acts: Infiltration, Escalation and Black Site
- Procedurally assembled facilities, so every new game plays differently
- Hidden intel fragments tell the story of Project LULLABY
- A multi-stage finale against the WARDEN, a three-phase boss fight followed by a lockdown escape
- An epilogue and credits worth reaching

UPGRADE YOUR CAT
Spend your credits in the rooftop Hideout on 12 cybernetic upgrades: armour, nano-repair, faster legs, phase dash, ghost step, optic camo, plasma rounds, extended mags, reflex boosters, suppressors, monofilament claws and a neural hack suite.

ENDLESS HEIST
Finished the campaign? Endless Heist keeps generating deeper, harder and better-paying contracts. How far can one cat go?

Fully original neon art, sound and synthwave music. Every visual and every sound is generated by the game itself.

## Properties / age rating guidance (Partner Center)

- **Category:** Games → Action & adventure (secondary: Shooter)
- **Privacy policy:** not required. The game doesn't collect or transmit
  personal data, uses no network connection, and only saves progress
  locally on the PC.
- **System requirements:** Windows 10 version 1809 or newer (x64); a GPU with
  OpenGL 3.3 support; 4 GB RAM; about 100 MB disk space; keyboard and mouse.
- **Age rating questionnaire (IARC), suggested answers:**
  - Violence: fantasy violence against robots/security units; no blood or gore
  - No sexual content, no strong language, no drugs, no gambling
  - No user interaction, no in-app purchases, no data sharing
  - Expected result: around PEGI 7 to 12 / ESRB E10+ to T (IARC decides)
- **Restricted capability `runFullTrust` justification** (if Partner Center
  asks): *"Kocur Neon Heist is a Win32 desktop game (Godot Engine)
  packaged with MSIX. runFullTrust is required to launch the game's desktop
  executable. The game does not access any user data, network or devices
  beyond the standard game window, audio and input."*
