## English (United States)

### Product name
Kocur Dungeon

### Short description [up to 1000 characters; shown at the top of the listing]
A small black cat against a whole dungeon. Claw, pounce and leap through 16
hand-crafted pixel-art levels, beat five guardian bosses and bring home the ten
friends the white-cat clan stole from your alley.

### Description [up to 10,000 characters]
Last night the white-cat clan of the Pale King crept out of the old city's
dungeon and carried away ten black cats from your alley. You are Kocur: small,
quick, and very, very angry.

Kocur Dungeon is a 2D pixel-art action platformer. Run, jump, claw and pounce
through five regions, each with its own enemies, hazards and a guardian waiting
at the end:

- Mossroot Catacombs: crumbling stones, fake walls, levers and gates.
- Emberpaw Mines: lava, flame jets, moving lifts and the archers of the clan.
- Frostfang Crypt: slippery ice, falling icicles, springs and spell-casting mages.
- Shadowclaw Depths: darkness lit only by torches, hidden passages and charging beasts.
- The Pale King's Citadel: saws, every enemy at once, a climb up the bell tower
  chased by rising pale fire, and a three-phase final duel with the Pale King himself.

Every attack is telegraphed, so every hit you take is a lesson. Read the wind-ups,
deflect arrows with your claws, pounce through danger with a moment of
invulnerability and punish the bosses when they leave themselves open.

Between levels you return to camp. Spend coins on seven permanent upgrades, choose
one of three temporary blessings for your next run, and watch every cat you
rescue join you by the campfire. Each rescued friend gives a permanent perk: an
extra heart, cheaper prices, more coins, tougher paws against spikes and lava,
and more.

Hunt for Shadow Gems and secrets, complete optional challenges in every level,
earn medals and best times, and push your completion to 100%.

- 16 levels in 5 regions, from a gentle tutorial to the Pale Throne
- 5 enemy types and 5 multi-phase guardian bosses
- Permanent upgrades, rescued-cat perks and temporary blessings
- Secrets, Shadow Gems, challenges, medals and a level select
- Assist, Normal and Hard difficulty
- Keyboard, mouse and gamepad support
- Original pixel art, music and sound
- Single player, fully offline; no account, no ads, no in-game purchases

### Popis
Minulú noc sa klan bielych mačiek Bledého kráľa vyplazil z podzemia starého
mesta a odniesol desať čiernych mačiek z tvojej uličky. Ty si Kocur: malý,

Kocur Dungeon je 2D akčná plošinovka v pixel-art štýle. Behaj, skáč, škrab
a vrhaj sa cez päť oblastí, z ktorých každá má vlastných nepriateľov, nástrahy
a strážcu na konci:

- Machové katakomby: drobiace sa kamene, falošné steny, páky a mreže.
- Žeravé bane: láva, plamene, pohyblivé výťahy a lukostrelci klanu.
- Mrazivá krypta: klzký ľad, padajúce cencúle, pružiny a čarujúci mágovia.
- Tieňové hlbiny: tma, ktorú osvetlia len fakle, skryté chodby a zúrivé šelmy.
- Citadela Bledého kráľa: píly, všetci nepriatelia naraz, výstup na zvonicu
  pred stúpajúcim bledým ohňom a trojfázový súboj so samotným Bledým kráľom.

Každý útok má viditeľný nápriah, takže každý zásah je lekcia. Sleduj nepriateľov,
odrážaj šípy pazúrmi, prevrhni sa cez nebezpečenstvo s krátkou nezraniteľnosťou
a trestaj strážcov, keď sa odkryjú.

Medzi úrovňami sa vraciaš do tábora. Za mince kupuješ sedem trvalých vylepšení,
vyberáš si jedno z troch dočasných požehnaní a každá zachránená mačka si k tebe
prisadne k ohňu. Každý zachránený kamarát dáva trvalý bonus: srdce navyše, nižšie
ceny, viac mincí, odolnejšie labky proti bodcom a láve a ďalšie.

Hľadaj Tieňové drahokamy a tajomstvá, plň voliteľné výzvy v každej úrovni,
zbieraj medaily a najlepšie časy a dotiahni hru na 100 %.

## runFullTrust justification (if Partner Center asks)
Kocur Dungeon is a desktop game built with the Godot Engine and distributed as a
Win32 executable (KocurDungeon.exe) packaged in MSIX. runFullTrust is required for
a desktop (Win32) application to run. The game does not access the network,
other apps' data or any personal data; it only reads and writes its own save
files in %APPDATA%\KocurDungeon.

## Notes for certification (Submission options)
Offline single-player game. No account, login or network access is required.
Controls: keyboard (A/D move, Space jump, J claw, K pounce, E interact, Esc pause),
mouse or Xbox gamepad.
