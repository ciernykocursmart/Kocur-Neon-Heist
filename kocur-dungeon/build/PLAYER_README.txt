KOCUR DUNGEON  v1.0.0  -  a Kocur Studios game
===============================================

A 2D pixel-art action platformer. You are Kocur, a small black cat. The
white-cat clan of the Pale King has stolen ten of your friends. Fight through
five regions, rescue the captives and defeat the Pale King.

HOW TO START
  1. Unzip the whole folder anywhere (for example to your Desktop).
  2. Double-click KocurDungeon.exe.
  Windows SmartScreen may warn about an unknown publisher because the
  executable is not code-signed: click "More info" -> "Run anyway".

REQUIREMENTS
  Windows 10/11 64-bit, a graphics card with OpenGL 3.3 support.
  No internet connection or installation needed.

CONTROLS (keyboard + mouse; gamepads also work)
  Move ............ A / D  or  Left / Right arrows
  Jump ............ Space  (hold for a higher jump)
  Claw attack ..... J  or  Left mouse button
  Pounce .......... K  or  Right mouse button  (hold W / Up to pounce upward)
  Drop down ....... hold S / Down + Space on thin platforms
  Interact ........ E  (doors, cages, levers, chests)
  Pause ........... Esc

SAVES AND SETTINGS
  %APPDATA%\KocurDungeon\save.json      (progress)
  %APPDATA%\KocurDungeon\settings.cfg   (volume, display, difficulty)
  Reset progress from Main Menu -> Settings -> Reset Progress.
